          How to read the results

          R is profit in units of risk. +1.5R means a trade hit TP1. -1R means it hit the stop. Avg R is the average per trade.

          Chance R is what random entries with the same stops and targets earned on this market. On Boom and Crash that is often negative, because spikes jump past stops.

          Edge is Avg R minus Chance R. z says how unlikely that edge is by luck. Below 2 is noise.

          Score runs from 0 to 100. About 27 is chance level, 65 or more is promising, and 85 or more needs a z of 3 with a positive edge in both halves of the history. Treat even a high score as something to forward test on demo, not a promise.
