        How to read these results

        Win is how often the price moved your way after the hold time. Chance is how often a random entry in the same direction would have won on this market. On Boom and Crash this is far from 50%, because price drifts between spikes.

        Edge is Win minus Chance. Avg move is the average result per trade in ATR units. A strategy can win often and still lose money if the losses are big, which is the usual Boom/Crash trap.

        z measures how unlikely the edge is by luck. Below 2 is noise. Because you are testing many strategies and markets, only trust z of 3 or more that also shows a positive edge in both halves of the history. That is the Possible edge verdict, and it still needs forward testing on demo.

            About

            Deriv's synthetic indices are generated by a random number generator. No indicator is proven to predict them. SynthScope shows what each tool says and measures, with the backtest, whether that has worked better than chance. It is not financial advice. Test on a demo account first and never trade money you cannot afford to lose.

            SynthScope reads public prices only. It never logs in to your account or places trades.
